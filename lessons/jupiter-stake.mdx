## Optimizing Capital via Jupiter Staking

In traditional finance, putting your capital to work often involves "management fees" and opaque processes. On Jupiter, capital optimization means direct, transparent, and low-cost participation in the network’s growth. 

<Term id="staking">Staking</Term> is one of the simplest ways to put your crypto to work. By staking your <Term id="sol">SOL</Term> with the Jupiter validator, you transform a static asset into a productive one, earning a share of network fees with a low 5% commission on inflation rewards and zero commission on MEV. Whether you choose the stability of Native <Term id="staking">Staking</Term> or the flexibility of Liquid <Term id="staking">Staking</Term> (JupSOL), you are choosing the most efficient path to grow your <Term id="solana">Solana</Term> holdings.

### What Is Staking?
<Term id="staking">Staking</Term> is a cornerstone of the <Term id="solana">Solana</Term> ecosystem. It serves as the primary mechanism for securing the network while rewarding participants for their capital commitment, by delegating <Term id="sol">SOL</Term> to a validator. Jupiter offers a high-performance validator designed to provide users with a secure, high-yield environment for their assets.

![Stake Interface](https://static.academy.jup.ag/images/illustrations/stake_interface.avif)

### Real-Time Yield & Performance
To ensure maximum profitability for participants, Jupiter maintains a highly competitive fee structure.

**Active Yield:** Variable, based on network conditions and validator performance. Check [jup.ag/stake](https://jup.ag/stake) for the current rate.

**Commissions:** Inflation 5.00% · MEV 0.00%

Jupiter charges a 5% commission on inflation rewards and takes 0% on MEV rewards, so nearly all of the validator’s earnings are passed directly to you. This keeps overhead well below the "service fee" costs typically found in traditional managed accounts.

![Manage Your Stake](https://static.academy.jup.ag/images/illustrations/stake_manage.avif)

### Key Concepts for Stake
* **The "Cool-Down" Period**. It is important to note that staked <Term id="sol">SOL</Term> is not instantly liquid. To support network stability, it takes approximately 2 days to unlock your funds once you request a withdrawal.  
* **The Validator**. A [validator](/lessons/consensus) is a [blockchain](/lessons/blockchain) participant that acts as the backbone of the network infrastructure. By choosing the Jupiter validator, you are opting for high-uptime hardware designed to maximize transaction throughput and reward frequency.

If you would rather not lock your SOL for the 2-day cool-down, Jupiter also offers liquid staking through [JupSOL](/lessons/jupiter-tokens), its liquid staking token. You receive JupSOL in exchange for your SOL and can trade it or use it in <Term id="defi">DeFi</Term> at any time while it keeps earning, though it carries an extra 5% fee on the base staking rewards, split between Sanctum (2.5%) and the Jupiter DAO treasury (2.5%).

## Let’s Stake with Jupiter

1. **Access Stake on your Jupiter App** — Open your wallet → top right corner “Browser” icon → go to [jup.ag/stake](https://jup.ag/stake).
2. **Delegate Your Principal** — Enter the amount you want to stake.
3. **Confirmation and Tracking** — Confirm the transaction → approve in your wallet.
